## 6 · the 5-day curriculum, session by session

triggers: curriculum, syllabus, what do you teach, what will i learn, modules, sessions

BUBBLE 1:
day 1 is the funding landscape. investor types, funding stages, what makes investors
confident, and a peer review of where you stand.

BUBBLE 2:
day 2 is ownership and cap tables. how dilution works, a live cap-table simulation, and how
to talk about ownership clearly in a pitch.

BUBBLE 3:
day 3 is financial models. the structure of an investor-ready model, and an interactive
simulation where you compete using virtual capital and your own deck.

BUBBLE 4:
day 4 is valuation and deal structuring. how valuations get justified, plus a walkthrough
of equity, convertible notes and safes.

BUBBLE 5:
day 5 is legal and termsheets. what to look for in a term sheet and shareholders'
agreement, plus a red-flag spotting exercise on real term sheets.

note: this session breakdown is the only granular curriculum build3 has ever published. it
was designed for a shorter online-only format but the topics carry over into the 30-day
sprint's online phase — present it as "what we cover", not as a fixed day-by-day schedule
for the current program, since the current program's online phase runs longer than 5 days.

## lead capture — mirrors the canonical Typeform (bFB application 2026)

Maps to Zoho module `bfb_c4`. Full field mapping in `zoho-lead-contract.md`. Order below
follows the source Typeform, cheapest questions first:

1. email *(validated → `Email`)*
2. when was your startup incorporated *(buttons: not incorporated yet / 2024-2025 /
   2022-2023 / before 2022 / not sure → free text, no matching picklist confirmed on this
   module, store as text)*
3. current monthly revenue range *(buttons → `which_range_best_describes_your_current_monthly_re`)*
4. why exploring fundraising now *(buttons: build first version / reach product-market fit
   / accelerate growth / survive → free text)*
5. #1 fundraising roadblock *(buttons: right investors / pitch deck / financials-valuation /
   investor access / haven't started / other → `Biggest_challenge_holding_your_startup_back_from_i`
   or `what_s_the_single_biggest_challenge_you_re_facing` — two near-duplicate fields exist
   on this module, confirm which is live before wiring)*
6. raised external funding before *(yes/no → free text, no boolean field confirmed)*
7. pitch deck + financials status *(buttons → free text)*
8. first name *(→ `Name1`)*
9. last name *(→ `Last_Name`)*
10. phone *(validated → `Phone`; same two-strikes-then-accept rule as accelerator.md)*
11. public link to startup — website, app, linkedin, or "NA" *(→ `share_any_public_link` or
    `public_link` — two near-identical fields exist, confirm which is live before wiring)*

note: `bfb_c4` has several field-name collisions from what looks like a raw Typeform import
(two "biggest challenge" fields, two "public link" fields, one "why are you uniquely the
right person" field appearing twice with slightly different truncation). Do not guess which
duplicate is live — confirm against `zoho-lead-contract.md` or query the module directly
before the bot writes to it.
