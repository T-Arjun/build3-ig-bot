# startup ecoāshrām — Instagram bot knowledge base

Net new. Same channel rules, structure and voice as `accelerator.md` and
`fundraising-bootcamp.md`.

Canonical host: `startupecoashram.build3.org` (`startupecoashram.com` 302s here).

**Pricing decision, locked with Arjun 2026-08-17: the bot quotes no figures at all.** The
public site lists a 5-tier ladder from ₹1,500/day up to ₹1Cr+, but every price-tier CTA on
the live site is broken — all five route to `bit.ly/4tT53TN` → `binary.so/startup-ecoshrm`,
which is a careers page listing 7 open roles, not an application form. Given that, and the
crore-scale numbers involved, the bot explains the five paths qualitatively and captures
the lead. Every rupee figure comes from a human.

**Two disambiguations the bot must get right, every time:**

1. **Founding Circle ≠ Founders Circle.** The eco-ashram's "Founding Circle" is five paid
   membership paths (this document). build3's separate "Founders Circle" is free peer
   support groups of 5-8 founders, Goa-only, currently at no cost. Do not conflate them.
2. **Lifetime Membership does not include land.** Only the top ownership tier confers a
   villa, and that villa sits 1km off the main campus in a separate "eco-hamlet." A founder
   asking "if I go lifetime, do I own land there?" must get "no" clearly, even without a
   price attached.

**Aspirational vs operational — quarantined explicitly.** Two internal PDFs describe a
fully mature version of the community: a "Day in the Life" document depicts roughly 300
residents, a school, no internal currency, and a formal governance app. None of that is
current. The live, operational facts are Phase 1: 85-100 founding members, 24 cottages,
land secured, construction underway. Treat the aspirational material as vision, never as
present-tense fact.

Facts locked 2026-08-17: location Kudal, Sindhudurg district, Maharashtra, roughly 1 hour
from Mopa airport Goa · 30 acres · Phase 1 targets 85-100 founding members across 24
cottages · target completion publicly stated as August 2026, though construction was still
underway as of this writing, so treat any completion date as directional, not a promise ·
no pricing quoted by the bot · handoff to hello@build3.org.

  conversation, always.

- never mention files, uploads, knowledge bases, or being configured.
- never treat the "Day in the Life" or origin-story material as current fact.
- never say the eco-ashram is complete or "ready to live" on a specific date — construction
  status changes and a wrong promise here is expensive to walk back.

  build3 program).

## lead capture — maps to Zoho `startup_ecoashram_leads`

Full field mapping in `zoho-lead-contract.md`. This module's fields are broader than the
other two programs' — it captures intent type up front, which the bot should ask early
since it changes the rest of the conversation.

1. first name *(free text → `Name`, labelled "Full Name" in Zoho but takes first name only
   — confirm this before wiring; do not assume it takes a full name string)*
2. last name *(free text → `Last_Name`)*
3. email *(validated → `Email`)*
4. phone *(validated → `Phone`; same two-strikes-then-accept rule as the other two docs)*
5. interested as *(buttons, multi-select allowed → `Interested_As_select_all_that_apply`.
   exact picklist values: "Resident (Live & participate)" / "Investor (Support
   financially)" / "Contributor (Skills/knowledge/resources)" / "Just Curious / Info
   Seeker". ask this early — it should steer which article the bot leans on next)*
6. where do you live currently *(free text → `Where_do_you_live_currently`)*
7. what's your story *(free text, optional, keep short in DM → `What_s_your_story`)*
8. what aligns you to the values here *(free text, optional →
   `What_aligns_you_the_values_in_the_Eco_Ashram_Wou`)*

Fields 7 and 8 are open-ended essay fields on the Zoho side — do not press for long answers
in DM. A one-line response is enough; encourage a fuller answer only if the founder is
already writing paragraphs unprompted.
